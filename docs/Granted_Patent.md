# Granted Patent – Retrospective-SPA Framework for GWAS and GxE methods

An invention patent related to the Retrospective Saddlepoint Approximation (Retrospective-SPA) framework for GWAS and GxE studies has been granted. This research centres on the original strategy of applying SPA to retrospective score statistics. The research programme includes:

The original strategy of combining retrospective score statistics with SPA, and its mathematical formulation (first conceived in 2021);

Its application to a wide range of complex phenotypes, including binary, quantitative, time-to-event, ordinal, longitudinal, and multivariate traits;

Robust handling of complex genetic architectures, such as population admixture (including local ancestry analysis with retrospective-SPA methods), cryptic relatedness, and rare-variant signals;

Integrated gene-environment interaction (G×E) testing under the retrospective framework, enabling accurate and scalable analysis across diverse study designs.

This retrospective–SPA strategy underpins a series of GWAS algorithms developed by Yuzhuo Ma and collaborators, including SPAmix, SPAmix+, SPAmix-QRS, SPAmix-QRS+, SPAGxE, SPAGxE+, SPAGxEmix, SPAGxEmix+, WtGxE, and their extensions. Please cite the relevant original work and retain applicable authorship and software-license notices when using these methods.
