# Granted Patent – Retrospective-SPA Framework for GWAS and GxE methods

This patent protects the methodology of the Retrospective Saddlepoint Approximation (Retrospective-SPA)​ framework — a unified statistical framework for genome-wide association studies (GWAS) and GxE studies. The invention covers:

The original conceptualization and mathematical formulation of the retrospective-SPA approach (first conceived in 2021);

Its application to a wide range of complex phenotypes, including binary, quantitative, time-to-event, ordinal, longitudinal, and multivariate traits;

Robust handling of complex genetic architectures, such as population admixture (including local ancestry analysis with retrospective-SPA methods), cryptic relatedness, and rare-variant signals;

Integrated gene-environment interaction (G×E) testing under the retrospective framework, enabling accurate and scalable analysis across diverse study designs.

This patented technology underpins a series of GWAS algorithms developed by the inventor, including SPAmix, SPAmix+, SPAGxE, SPAmix-QRS, SPAmix-QRS+, and their extensions. The patent establishes the legal priority and intellectual property ownership of the retrospective-SPA framework and its derivatives.
